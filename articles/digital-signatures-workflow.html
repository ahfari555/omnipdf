Digital Signatures & Workflow Security

1,200 Words • Published 2026

Introduction to Electronic and Digital Signatures

In modern digital document management, ensuring the authenticity and non-repudiation of agreements is paramount. Digital signatures utilize Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) to bind a signer's identity to a specific document securely.

Cryptographic Verification in PDFs

Unlike simple scanned images of handwritten signatures, cryptographic digital signatures embed a mathematical hash code into the PDF structure. Any unauthorized modification to the file after signing will instantly invalidate the signature, ensuring total document integrity.
