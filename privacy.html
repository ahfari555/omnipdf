Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

At OmniPDF, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by OmniPDF and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Document Processing

All PDF manipulations (including merging, image conversion, and rotating pages) occur 100% locally on your browser using WebAssembly and Web APIs. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server or stored in any database.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

OmniPDF uses standard cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accesses.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about our Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out via our Contact Page.
